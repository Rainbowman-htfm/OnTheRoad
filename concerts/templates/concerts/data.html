Your data

For bands

  • Your concert dates are public. Like any concert announcement: dates, venues, times, ticket links and support acts appear on your page, your widget, your feeds and in search engines.
  • They stay yours. Change or delete a date at any time: your page, your widget and your feeds follow, and so do your fans' calendars. Deleting your band deletes its pages.
  • Your dates are always available in open formats, to take them elsewhere or feed other tools:
    • /<your-band>/concerts.ics: every concert, past ones included, in the standard calendar format;
    • /<your-band>/events.json: upcoming concerts in the schema.org format used by search engines.
  • Your account stays private. The email address you log in with, and which bands you belong to, are never published, sold or shared with partners.

For fans

  • No account, no email. You follow a band by adding its calendar or feed; we don't know who you are.
  • No tracking. No cookies on public pages, no analytics, no third-party scripts, fonts or images. The only cookie is the login of band members, in the space where they manage their dates.
