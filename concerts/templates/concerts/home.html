Follow a band in one tap

Tap "Add to my calendar" on a band's page and their upcoming shows will appear in your phone's calendar — and stay up to date when dates change.

No account to create. No email to give. We don't know who you are, and we like it that way.

For bands

Enter your dates once. You get:

  • a page to put in your Linktree or bio,
  • a widget for your own website,
  • a calendar feed your fans can follow,
  • structured data so search engines show your dates.

Your data is yours: we never sell it or share it with partners. Like any concert announcement, your dates are public.

Our principles

  • No account for fans, no tracking. No trackers, no third-party services loaded by our pages. Only bands have an account, to manage their dates.
  • Lightweight. Static pages of a few kilobytes: fast even in Instagram's in-app browser, with a minimal footprint.
  • Independent. No ads, no data sold. Concert dates are public information; we don't monetise them.
  • Open. Free software under the AGPL licence: read the code.
